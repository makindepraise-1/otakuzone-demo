Attack on titan(Shingeki no Kyoji) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama . Set in a world where humanity is forced to live in cities surrounded by three ernomous walls that protect them from gigantic man-eating humanoids referred to as Titans, the story follows Eren Yeager , an adolecent boy who vows to exterminate the Titans after they bring about the destruction of his hometown and the death of his mother. It was serialized in Koshida's monthly magazine "Bessatsu Shonen Magazine" from September 2009 to April 2021, with its chapters collected in 34 tankobon volumes.

Synopsis

Attack on Titan (Shingeki no Kyojin) is set in a world where humanity teeters on the brink of extinction, forced to live within enormous walled cities to protect themselves from giant humanoid creatures known as Titans. These mysterious beings appear without warning and devour humans seemingly without reason, driving the remnants of civilization into a constant state of fear and desperation.

The story follows Eren Yeager, a passionate and impulsive young man whose life is shattered when the outermost wall is breached by a colossal Titan, leading to the destruction of his hometown and the tragic death of his mother. Driven by a burning desire for revenge and a longing for freedom, Eren enlists in the military alongside his adoptive sister Mikasa Ackerman and childhood friend Armin Arlert. Together, they join the fight against the Titans as members of the elite Survey Corps, an organization dedicated to venturing beyond the walls and uncovering the truth behind the Titans' origins.

As the trio faces the horrors of battle, they encounter powerful allies and formidable enemies, including the legendary soldier Levi Ackerman and the enigmatic Commander Erwin Smith. The series delves into the psychological and moral struggles of its characters as they confront betrayal, loss, and the harsh realities of war. Over time, Eren and his friends begin to unravel the secrets of the Titans, discovering shocking truths about their own society, the history of the walls, and the nature of humanity itself.

Attack on Titan is renowned for its intense action sequences, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes of survival, freedom, and the cyclical nature of violence. As the battle between humans and Titans escalates, the line between friend and foe blurs, and the fate of humanity hangs in the balance. The series ultimately challenges viewers to question the true meaning of freedom and the cost of pursuing it in a world filled with fear and uncertainty.

Main Characters

  • Eren Yeager: The main protagonist who seeks revenge against the Titans.
  • Mikasa Ackerman: Eren's adoptive sister and a skilled soldier.
  • Armin Arlert: Eren's childhood friend known for his intelligence.
  • Levi Ackerman: A legendary soldier known for his exceptional combat skills.
